### Certification

Climbing gyms in Peru must be certified by recognized organizations such as UIAA and IFM. This ensures that the gym meets certain safety standards and has a track record of operating safely.

### Supervision

Monitors at these gyms should receive formal training in first aid and rescue techniques to ensure they are equipped to handle emergency situations.

### Equipment

The equipment used by climbing gyms in Peru must be suitable for the conditions and terrain. This includes adequate protection gear, harnesses, ropes, and other necessary equipment.

### Emergency Plan

A comprehensive emergency plan should be in place to respond to situations of risk, such as avalanches or earthquakes. This plan should outline procedures for evacuation, rescue, and communication.
